What only store optimization explains
✓ CVR 0.19% → 0.40% (visitor-to-buyer rate)

The decisive test: July 2025 — also during elevated SSD demand — had 163k sessions and produced only 187 orders. July 2026 had 60k sessions and produced 239 orders. If the market was the driver, the high-traffic month should have won. It didn't. That reversal is the GMDS effect.
